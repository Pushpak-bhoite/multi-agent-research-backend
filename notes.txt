What you can now say in the interview

You have three different technologies doing three different jobs:

Pandas

"I used Pandas to preprocess the structured search results, remove duplicate URLs, sort the results and select the top sources."

NLP

"I implemented an NLP-based relevance layer using TF-IDF and cosine similarity. It converts the research topic and scraped documents into vectors and calculates their similarity, allowing us to prioritize the most relevant documents."

LangGraph

"I used LangGraph to orchestrate the complete multi-agent workflow and maintain shared state between the search, reader, NLP, writer and critic nodes."

And importantly, don't say TF-IDF is an LLM or AI model.

It's a classical NLP technique:

TF-IDF
   ↓
text → numerical representation

Cosine Similarity
   ↓
two vectors → similarity score
